## 1. Core Framework: Covenant Theology
Horton is one of the premier modern defenders of **Covenant Theology** (Federalism). He views the entire Bible through the lens of divine covenants.

### The Three Covenants:
1.  **Covenant of Redemption (Pactum Salutis)**: An eternal agreement within the Trinity (Father, Son, Spirit) for the salvation of the elect.
2.  **Covenant of Works (Foedus Operum)**: Made with Adam in the Garden. Adam failed to provide perfect obedience as our federal head.
3.  **Covenant of Grace (Foedus Gratiae)**: Established after the Fall, where God provides through Jesus Christ what Adam failed to provide. Christ is the "Last Adam" who fulfills the Covenant of Works on our behalf.

---

## 2. "Christless Christianity" & The Gospel
Horton warns against **Moralistic Therapeutic Deism (MTD)**, where the church focuses on "how-to" tips for a better life rather than the objective work of Christ.
- **The Gospel is News, not Advice**: The Gospel is the report of what God has already done in history, not a list of things we must do.
- **Law and Gospel Distinction**: Correctly distinguishing between what God commands (Law) and what God gives (Gospel) is central to Horton's theology.

---

## 3. Justification (The Great Exchange)
- **Forensic Justification**: God's legal declaration that the sinner is righteous, based solely on the imputed righteousness of Christ.
- **Simul Iustus Et Peccator**: At the same time righteous (in Christ) and a sinner (in ourselves).
