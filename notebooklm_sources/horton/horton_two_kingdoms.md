# Michael Horton: The Two Kingdoms & Public Theology

Michael Horton, following the magisterial Reformation tradition (specifically the Reformed/Calvinist line), advocates for a **Two Kingdoms** view of the world.

### 1. The Kingdom of Christ (Redemptive Kingdom)
- **Nature**: The spiritual kingdom where Christ rules over His people through the Word and Sacraments.
- **Location**: Manifested primarily in the visible Church.
- **Method**: The "Sword of the Spirit" (Preaching and teaching).
- **Goal**: Eternal salvation and the formation of the New Creation.

### 2. The Kingdom of Culture (Common Grace Kingdom)
- **Nature**: The temporal kingdom where Christ rules over all creation through common grace, natural law, and civil government.
- **Location**: The workplace, politics, education, family, and the arts.
- **Method**: The "Sword of the Magistrate" (Civil law and secular authority).
- **Goal**: Temporal peace, justice, and the flourishing of human society.

### Key Insights for Modern Christians:
- **No Hierarchical Dualism**: Secular work is not "lesser" than ministry. Everything is under Christ's rule, but in different ways.
- **Against Transformationism**: Horton is wary of "Christianizing" the secular world through Power. Instead, he advocates for being "faithful witnesses" in our specific vocations.
- **Common Ground**: Christians and non-Christians can work together for the common good in the Kingdom of Culture based on shared moral principles (Natural Law).
