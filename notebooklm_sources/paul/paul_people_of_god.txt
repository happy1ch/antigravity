N.T. Wright: "One of the most misunderstood parts of Paul's letters is his ecclesiology. We often treat the 'Church' as a secondary topic, but for Paul, the Church—the 'People of God'—is the actual evidence of the New Creation. In Ephesians and Colossians, the fact that people from different races and social classes can sit at the same table and call each other 'brother' and 'sister' is what shatters the powers and principalities of this world."
