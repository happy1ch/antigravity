# [Theo-Stream] N.T. Wright: Pauline Theology Corpus Summary
**Target**: Pauline Theology Source for NotebookLM  
**Primary Scholar**: N.T. Wright (Bishop of Durham, Emeritus)

---

## 1. Core Thesis: "The Transformative Gospel"
Paul did not just write letters; he **invented Christian Theology** by combining Jewish Messianic expectations with Greek philosophical rigor and Roman social context. 

### Key Insights from "How Paul Invented Christian Theology":
- Paul adapted the 'Shema' (Deu 6:4) to include Jesus Christ at the heart of the one God's identity.
- This was not an abandonment of Judaism but its fulfillment.
- The Gospel is not just "how to get to heaven" but "Jesus is Lord, and Caesar is not."

---

## 2. The New Perspective on Paul (NPP)
- **Justification**: It is not just about individuals being saved from sin, but about the creation of a **new, multi-ethnic family** (The People of God) promised to Abraham.
- **Faithfulness (Pistis Christou)**: Focusing on Christ’s faithfulness as the mechanism of salvation, rather than just the believer's subjective faith.

---

## 3. Trinitarian Theology in Paul (1 Corinthians 8:6)
- Paul's 'Christological Monotheism': Integrating the Son and Spirit into the one Godhead without breaking monotheism.
- The 'Spirit' is the one who enables the community to groan with creation (Romans 8).

## 5. Modern Application: 'Pauline Power'
Wright argues that Paul's vision challenges modern individualism. For a modern worker or student, Pauline theology offers a "Third Way" between isolation and absorption into the state—the community of the Church as a sign of the New Creation.
